This is a normal paragraph. Write in Markdown; the site turns it into HTML when it builds. Line
breaks inside a paragraph do not matter, so you can wrap the text however you like in the editor.

To start a real post, copy this file, give it a new filename, and change the five lines at the top.
The filename becomes the URL: `src/posts/who-owns-the-water.md` is published at `/who-owns-the-water/`.

## A subheading looks like this

Use `##` for section headings and `###` for anything below that. Keep them descriptive — they are
how someone skims a long argument.

Links are [written like this](https://example.com) and come out in red. **Bold** works, *italic*
works, and `inline code` works.

> A quotation gets a red rule down its left side and is set in the serif face. Use it for something
> somebody actually said, not for emphasising your own sentence.

A list, when the content really is a list:

- One point per line.
- Sentence case, no full stop needed on fragments.
- Three to five items before it starts feeling like a checklist.
